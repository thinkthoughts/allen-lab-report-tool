# Research Proposal - Allen Lab Collaboration

## Core Idea
Apply Residue Manifold Learning (RML) and Constraint-Guided Coherence Scoring (CGCS) to quantify how an extra copy of chromosome 21 disrupts biological coherence and how interventions restore it.

## Key Innovation
- Treat chromosome 21 gene dosage as residue streams on a modular manifold
- Use CGCS as a multiplicative metric for structural fidelity under dosage noise
- Model therapeutic interventions as projection/recovery operations

## Alignment with Allen Lab
- Transcriptional dysregulation
- RNA biology and propagation of effects
- Person-to-person variability
- Intervention evaluation

## Current Status
- Base RML framework exists (see ../residue-manifold-learning)
- Adapted CGCS formula for aneuploidy defined
- Initial mapping of DNA base-pairing to modular residues completed
